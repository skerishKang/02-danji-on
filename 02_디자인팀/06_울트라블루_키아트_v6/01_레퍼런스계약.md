## 2. Reference grammar that must remain recognizable

1. Flat, restrained header over a full-width key scene.
2. Hero divided into two strong photographic/color fields, with headline bridging the visual tension.
3. Immediate transition from hero to a horizontally browsable primary-content rail.
4. Centered editorial section with three differently weighted image stories.
5. Six compact need categories followed by a quiet numerical evidence band.
6. Alternating large photographic feature bands instead of repeated equal cards.
7. Dark high-contrast need-selection scene with stable content replacement.
8. Three vertical resident-story panels, one quotation scene, one slow cinematic image scene.
9. Three journal cards, a short pilot/subscribe band, and an image-strip footer.
10. Large-to-small-to-large scroll rhythm and clear background transitions.

## 3. Danjion translation constraints

- Product → resident-run shop or service.
- Product photography → hands, tools, workspace, work result.
- Product efficacy → what the resident does and how it helps.
- Purchase → search, inquiry method, resident benefit, save.
- Brand story → the work of a neighbor living in the same complex.
- Membership benefit → verified resident-only benefit.

## 4. Non-negotiable product constraints

- `방림명지로드힐`, resident verification, search, both main CTAs must appear immediately.
- Search and CTAs are usable before animation completes.
- Mobile search is visible in the initial 390×844 viewport.
- Body text ≥16px, primary buttons ≥52px, touch target ≥48px.
- Five labeled bottom-navigation items remain on mobile.
- No scroll hijacking, auto audio, autoplay carousel, 3D rotation, or hidden core function.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` preserves all information and controls.

## 5. Motion lock

- Hero mask reveal: 680ms.
- Headline line reveal: 550ms, 100ms stagger.
- Hero photo: 8s, maximum 5% scale.
- Stable panel replacement: 170–190ms fade out, total under 340ms.
- Rail movement: browser smooth scroll, approximately 320ms perceived.
- Save/complete feedback: 150–220ms.
- Section reveal: 620ms; disabled under reduced motion.
